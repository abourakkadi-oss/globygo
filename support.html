How do I add a country or city?

Tap + and start typing, for example "mar" for Morocco or Marrakech. Pick a result and choose Been, Lived or Want to go. You can also tap countries on the map.

How do family profiles work?

Each family member has their own passport on the same phone. Tap the profile chip at the top to switch, or add a new member there.

How do levels work?

Levels follow the countries you've visited: Backpacker at 5, Voyager at 15, Globetrotter at 30, Trailblazer at 60 and Legend at 100.

Will I lose my data if I update the app?

No. Updates keep your places. Your data is stored on your phone and included in your device backups.
