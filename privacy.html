Globy Go

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Globy Go keeps your travels on your phone. We don't collect, store or share any of your data, there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

The countries and cities you add, your ratings and notes, and your family profiles (names, emoji and colours) are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on, under your Apple account and Apple's privacy terms.

What we collect

Nothing. Globy Go has no sign-in and no server. It doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos or camera.

Network use

The maps, flags, city list and travel photos are built into the app, so it works fully offline. The app doesn't send your information anywhere.

Children

Families can add child profiles, which work like any other profile: they stay on the device and are never sent to us or anyone else. We don't knowingly collect any information from children, or from anyone else.

TestFlight

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. That feedback is used only to fix and improve the app.

Deleting your data

You can remove a place or a profile at any time in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If future versions add features such as cloud sync or sharing between family phones, we'll update this policy before they ship and change the date above.
